Our Story

Born from a vision of belonging

The Arab Club at APU began as a simple yet powerful idea — Arab students deserve a space where they feel seen, supported, and celebrated.

The idea emerged between April and May, officially approved on October 30, and brought to life by its determined founder. Today it stands as the central hub for academic growth, cultural connection, and community development for Arab students across APU.

Raghad
Founder & President

Founder and President of the Arabic Club at Asia Pacific University, dedicated to promoting Arabic culture, academic excellence, and a supportive student community.

Direction

Vision, Mission
& Purpose

Our Purpose
"To support Arab students academically, create a community that strengthens their connection and identity, and provide an environment for development, communication, and cultural pride within APU."
👁️
Our Vision

To become a strong, influential Arab community that fosters a deep sense of belonging and Arab identity — not just within APU, but beyond the campus walls.

🎯
Our Mission
  • Empower Arab students through dedicated academic support and guidance
  • Organize diverse events — educational, cultural, social, and sports
  • Build a network that develops members' skills and presence on and off campus
